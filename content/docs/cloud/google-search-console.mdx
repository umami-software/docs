Connect Google Search Console to a website to see which Google searches bring visitors to it. Umami shows your search
queries, clicks, impressions, click-through rate, and average position next to the rest of your analytics.

Google Search Console is an [integration](/docs/cloud/integrations) and requires a Pro or Business plan. Umami reads
search data from Google whenever you open the report. Nothing is copied or stored in Umami.

## Before you begin

Your website must be a verified property in [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console). You will
connect with the Google account that has access to that property.

## Connect Google Search Console

Log into Umami and click on the side nav profile button then click **Settings**.

![image](/images/cloud/navbar-settings.png)

Navigate to **Integrations**, click on the **Add integration** button, and choose **Google Search Console**.

![image](/images/cloud/integration-add.png)

Select the **Website** to show search data for. This can't be changed later. Click **Continue with Google**, choose
your Google account, and grant Umami access to Search Console.

![image](/images/cloud/integration-search-console-form.png)

You will be returned to the **Integrations** page. If Umami can't choose a property for your website, click on the
**Edit** button next to the integration and select the **Search Console property** that matches your website.

## Viewing search data

Navigate to a website and click the **Searches** tab.

![image](/images/cloud/search-console-report.png)

| Metric | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Clicks | Number of clicks from Google search results to your website. |
| Impressions | Number of times your website appeared in Google search results. |
| CTR | Click-through rate, the percentage of impressions that resulted in a click. |
| Average position | Average position of your website in Google search results. |

Search data can be broken down by **Queries**, **Pages**, **Countries**, and **Devices**.

<Callout title="Note">

Search data comes from Google, so it is reported in whole days and recent days may not be available yet. Filters
don't apply to search data.

</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
| --- | --- |
| No properties are listed | The Google account you connected has no verified Search Console properties. Disconnect the integration and connect again with the Google account that has access to your website in Search Console. |
| Google did not grant lasting access | Remove Umami from your Google account's [third-party access](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) and connect again. |
| The report asks you to reconnect | Google rejected the connection. Disconnect the integration and connect it again. |

## Disconnect Google Search Console

Navigate to **Integrations** and click on the **Disconnect** button next to the integration. Search data will no
longer be shown for the website. To revoke access, remove Umami from your Google account's
[third-party access](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
