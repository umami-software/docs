---
title: Payment Providers
---

Connect your Stripe account to a website to see your recurring revenue in Umami. Umami imports your
Stripe invoices and shows your ARR (annual recurring revenue) over time, broken down into new,
retained, resurrected, expansion, contraction, and churned revenue, along with year-over-year growth
and an ARR roll-forward table.

Setting up a payment provider takes three steps:

1. [Create a read-only API key](#create-a-read-only-api-key) in Stripe.
2. [Add the payment provider](#add-a-payment-provider) in Umami.
3. Optionally, [set up a webhook](#set-up-live-updates-optional) for live updates.

## Create a read-only API key

Umami only needs to **read** your invoices, so always use a Stripe **restricted key** with read-only
permissions. Never use your secret key (`sk_live_...`), which has full access to your Stripe
account.

<Callout title="Use a read-only key" variant="warning">

A restricted key limits what Umami can do in your Stripe account. With read-only permissions, the
key can't create charges, issue refunds, or change any data, even if it were exposed.

</Callout>

In the Stripe Dashboard, go to **Developers** → **API keys** and click **Create restricted key**.

Give the key a name, such as `Umami`, then set the following permissions to **Read**:

| Resource | Permission |
| --- | --- |
| Invoices | Read |
| Events | Read |
| Prices | Read |

Leave every other permission set to **None**, then click **Create key**. Copy the key, which starts
with `rk_live_`.

## Add a payment provider

Log into Umami and click on the side nav profile button then click **Settings**.

![image](/images/cloud/navbar-settings.png)

Navigate to **Payment providers** and click on the **Add payment provider** button.

Fill out the form:

- **Name**: a name to identify this provider, for example `Stripe`.
- **Website**: the website this revenue belongs to.
- **Provider**: `Stripe`.
- **API key**: the restricted key you created above.

Click **Save**. Umami immediately starts importing your full invoice history. Depending on how many
invoices you have, this can take a few minutes. Click on the provider's name to view your revenue.

## Keeping data up to date

Umami refreshes your invoice data from Stripe **every 4 hours**. Each refresh picks up new invoices
and changes to existing ones, such as an unpaid invoice that has since been paid or an invoice that
was voided.

To refresh right away, open the payment provider and click **Sync now**. The time of the last sync is
shown next to the button.

## Set up live updates (optional)

If you want changes in Stripe to appear in Umami within seconds instead of on the next refresh, add a
webhook in Stripe. This is optional. Without it, your data still refreshes every 4 hours.

### 1. Copy your webhook URL

In Umami, navigate to **Payment providers** and click the **Edit** button next to your provider.
Copy the **Webhook URL**. Each payment provider has its own URL.

### 2. Create the webhook in Stripe

In the Stripe Dashboard, go to **Developers** → **Webhooks** and add an endpoint:

- **Endpoint URL**: the webhook URL you copied from Umami.
- **Events to send**: select these events:
  - `invoice.created`
  - `invoice.finalized`
  - `invoice.paid`
  - `invoice.payment_failed`
  - `invoice.updated`
  - `invoice.voided`
  - `invoice.marked_uncollectible`

Save the endpoint, then reveal and copy its **Signing secret**, which starts with `whsec_`.

### 3. Add the signing secret to Umami

Back in Umami, paste the signing secret into the **Webhook secret** field of your payment provider
and click **Save**. Umami uses the secret to verify that webhook requests come from Stripe.

## Edit or delete a payment provider

Navigate to **Payment providers** and click on the **Edit** button or **Delete** button next to your
payment provider. When editing, leave the **API key** or **Webhook secret** field blank to keep the
current value.

<Callout title="Note">

Deleting a payment provider in Umami doesn't change anything in your Stripe account. If you set up a
webhook, delete it in Stripe under **Developers** → **Webhooks**, and roll or delete the restricted
key under **Developers** → **API keys**.

</Callout>
