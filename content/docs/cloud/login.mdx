---
title: Login
description: Log into your Umami Cloud account, reset a forgotten password, and change your password from your account settings.
---

You can log into your account at [https://cloud.umami.is/login](https://cloud.umami.is/login?ref=umami-cloud-docs).

![image](/images/cloud/login-form.png)

If you forget your password click the **Forgot password?** button and enter your email to receive an email to reset your password.

![image](/images/cloud/login-forgot-password.png)

You can change your password by clicking on the side nav profile button, clicking **Settings**, navigating to **Account**, and clicking the **Change password** button.

![image](/images/cloud/account-manage.png)
