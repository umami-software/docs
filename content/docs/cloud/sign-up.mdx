---
title: Sign up
description: Sign up for a free Umami Cloud account, verify your email address, and complete the account setup by selecting your data region.
---

You can sign up for free at [https://cloud.umami.is/signup](https://cloud.umami.is/signup?ref=umami-cloud-docs).

## Verify email address

After signing up you will receive a 6-digit code to verify your email address.

![image](/images/cloud/sign-up-verify-email.png)

## Account setup

Log into your account to reach the setup page. You must complete the steps to **Verify email address** and **Select data region**.
Choose the US or EU data region. See [Cloud data regions](/docs/cloud/faq#where-are-your-servers-located) for how to check your selection, its scope, and questions about changing regions.

You can walk through the setup guide or press the **Skip setup** button at the bottom of the screen to continue to Umami Cloud.

![image](/images/cloud/sign-up-setup.png)
