---
title: FAQ
description: Answers to common questions about Umami, including GDPR compliance, cookies, collected data, self-hosting, supported databases, and data retention.
---

### 1. Is Umami GDPR compliant?

Umami is designed with privacy in mind. GDPR compliance depends on how you configure and use it.

### 2. Do I need to display a cookie notice to users?

Umami's web tracker does not use cookies. Whether your website needs a cookie notice depends on the other services you use and applicable requirements.

### 3. Does Umami work on a single page application (SPA)?

Yes, Umami automatically tracks page navigations in SPAs without any additional configuration.

### 4. Can Umami record events such as button clicks?

Yes, please see [Track Events](/docs/track-events) under **Tracking**.

### 5. What data does Umami collect?

Umami collects pageviews, referrer URLs, browsers, operating systems, device types, and approximate location. See [Metric definitions](/docs/metric-definitions) for a full list.

### 6. Can I self-host Umami?

Yes, Umami is open source and can be self-hosted on your own infrastructure. See [Installation](/docs/install) for details.

### 7. What databases does Umami support?

Umami supports PostgreSQL (minimum v12.14).

### 8. How long is data retained?

For self-hosted installations, data is retained indefinitely unless you manually delete it. For Umami Cloud, see the [Cloud FAQ](/docs/cloud/faq).

### 9. Does Umami support product analytics?

Yes. Umami supports product analytics with features such as [custom events](/docs/track-events), [funnels](/docs/funnel), [retention](/docs/retention), and [cohorts](/docs/cohorts).

### 10. Can I query Umami from an AI assistant?

Yes. Umami provides an official, read-only [MCP server](/docs/mcp) for clients such as Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor.

### 11. Where can I go for help if I have problems or want to request a feature?

For general discussions and troubleshooting you can use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/umami-software/umami/discussions).

For bug reports and feature requests, please file an [issue](https://github.com/umami-software/umami/issues) on GitHub.

For the most fun, join the Umami community on [Discord](https://discord.gg/4dz4zcXYrQ)!
