---
title: Annotations
---

*Available since v3.4*

**Annotations** let you pin a note to a date on a website's traffic chart. Use
them to record things that explain a change in the numbers, like a deploy, a
campaign launch, an outage, or a mention in the press. Next time you or a
teammate opens the chart, the note is sitting right on the day it happened.

In the dashboard, the button is labeled **Notes**.

## Adding an Annotation

Open a website and on the Overview page, find the website chart. Below it, on the
right, click **Notes**.

![image](/images/docs/annotation-chart.png)

In the dialog, click **Add note**, then:

1. Pick the date the note applies to.
2. Type the note text. You get up to 500 characters.
3. Click **Save**.

![image](/images/docs/annotation-add.png)

You need edit access to the website to add, edit, or delete annotations. On
shared and public dashboards they show up but can't be changed.

## Chart Markers

Every annotation shows up as a marker on the chart, lined up with the point where
its date falls. If more than one annotation lands on the same point, they combine
into a single marker with a count on it.

![image](/images/docs/annotation-marker.png)

## Tooltip

Hover over a marker to see what's on it: the date and text for up to three notes.
If a marker groups more than three, a **View** button opens the full list for
that point.

![image](/images/docs/annotation-tooltip.png)

## Drilling In

Click a marker to pull the whole dashboard onto that annotation's date. Every
metric and panel updates to cover just that stretch of time. On a monthly chart a
grouped marker takes you to the month, and on a daily chart it takes you to the
day.

You can drill in when the chart covers more than one day and isn't already in
hourly view.

## Managing Annotations

The **Notes** dialog lists your annotations in a table.

- Use the toggle to switch between **Current Date range** and **All Notes**.
- Click the date in a row to drill into that annotation's date.
- Use the row actions to edit or delete an annotation. This needs edit access.

![image](/images/docs/annotation-details.png)
