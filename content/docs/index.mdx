Umami is a web analytics platform that brings traffic, campaigns, visitor behavior, conversions, and revenue together in one place. It gives you a complete picture of how people find and use your website, from their first visit to the outcomes that matter to your business.

These docs cover **Umami v3**. For the previous version, see the [v2 documentation](https://v2.umami.is).

![Umami analytics dashboard](/images/docs/screenshot.png)

## Understand your website from end to end

- **Measure acquisition** — See pageviews, visitors, referrers, locations, devices, and real-time activity. Track [UTM campaigns](/docs/utm), [links](/docs/links), and [pixels](/docs/pixels) to understand which channels bring people to your site.
- **Analyze behavior** — Go beyond pageviews with [custom events](/docs/track-events), [journeys](/docs/journey), [session replays](/docs/replays), and [heatmaps](/docs/heatmaps). Use [segments](/docs/segments), [cohorts](/docs/cohorts), and [retention](/docs/retention) to examine the audiences and patterns behind your metrics.
- **Improve conversion** — Define the outcomes you care about with [goals](/docs/goals), build [funnels](/docs/funnel) to find drop-off points, and use [attribution](/docs/attribution) to connect conversions to the marketing that influenced them.
- **Connect traffic to revenue** — Attach values to events and use the [revenue report](/docs/revenue) to see which pages, campaigns, and visitor segments generate the most value.
- **Share and extend your analytics** — Create focused dashboards with [boards](/docs/using-boards), collaborate through [teams](/docs/using-teams), share website statistics, or use the [API](/docs/api) to build your own reports and integrations.

## Privacy without sacrificing insight

Umami provides these capabilities without cookies, cross-site tracking, or the automatic collection of personal data. You can use the managed cloud service or self-host Umami on your own infrastructure, keeping control over where your analytics data lives.

## Get started

Choose the setup that fits your team:

- **Umami Cloud** — [Create a free account](https://cloud.umami.is/signup?ref=umami-docs-introduction) and let us manage hosting, updates, backups, and scaling.
- **Self-hosted** — Follow the [installation guide](/docs/install) to run the open-source platform on your own infrastructure, or choose one of the [deployment guides](/docs/guides/hosting).

Once Umami is running:

1. [Add your website](/docs/add-a-website).
2. [Install the tracking script](/docs/collect-data).
3. Open your website dashboard and start exploring your data.
