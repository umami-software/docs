## What is Umami?

Umami is an open-source web and product analytics platform. It measures how people find your website or product, what they do there, whether they convert, and whether they come back.

Umami collects data without cookies and does not collect personal data automatically. You can use the managed service, [Umami Cloud](/docs/cloud), or self-host the open-source platform on your own infrastructure and keep full control of your data.

## What you can use it for

- **Understand acquisition.** See pageviews, visitors, referrers, channels, locations, and devices, and measure campaigns with [UTM parameters](/docs/utm), [links](/docs/links), [pixels](/docs/pixels), and [attribution](/docs/attribution).
- **Analyze behavior.** Track actions with [custom events](/docs/track-events) and event properties, explore the paths people take with [journeys](/docs/journey), and inspect individual [sessions](/docs/sessions).
- **Measure conversion.** Define [goals](/docs/goals), build [funnels](/docs/funnel) to find where people drop off, and connect outcomes to [revenue](/docs/revenue).
- **Measure retention.** Use the [retention](/docs/retention) report to see how often visitors return, and compare groups with [segments](/docs/segments) and [cohorts](/docs/cohorts).
- **Investigate interactions.** Watch [session replays](/docs/replays) and view click and scroll [heatmaps](/docs/heatmaps).
- **Monitor performance.** Track Core Web Vitals with the [performance](/docs/performance) report.
- **Share and extend.** Build dashboards with [boards](/docs/using-boards), collaborate through [teams](/docs/using-teams), add context with [annotations](/docs/annotations), use the [API](/docs/api), or connect an AI assistant through the official [MCP server](/docs/mcp).

## Does Umami support product analytics?

Yes. Custom events, funnels, goals, journeys, retention, segments, and cohorts are built in and use the same tracker as web analytics.

By default, Umami identifies visitors with an anonymous session identifier and no cookies, so a person who changes device or browser appears as a new visitor. To connect the sessions of a logged-in user, set a [distinct ID](/docs/distinct-ids).

Umami does not include feature flags, surveys, or a built-in experimentation engine. You can measure A/B tests by [tagging variants](/docs/guides/setup-ab-testing).

## Umami Cloud and self-hosted

The open-source platform and Umami Cloud share the same analytics features. They differ in who runs the infrastructure, and some Cloud features depend on your plan.

| Capability | Umami Cloud | Self-hosted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Web analytics, custom events, and event properties | All plans | Included |
| Funnels, goals, journeys, retention, UTM, revenue, and attribution | All plans | Included |
| Segments and cohorts | All plans | Included (v3.0 or later) |
| Boards | All plans | Included (v3.1 or later) |
| Teams | Pro, Business, and Enterprise | Included |
| API access | Pro, Business, and Enterprise | Included |
| [MCP](/docs/mcp) | Pro, Business, and Enterprise | Included (v3.4 or later, disabled by default) |
| Session replays | Business and Enterprise | Included (v3.1 or later) |
| Heatmaps | Business and Enterprise | Included (v3.2 or later) |
| [Email reports](/docs/cloud/email-reports) | Pro, Business, and Enterprise | Cloud only |
| Hosting, updates, backups, and scaling | Managed by Umami | Managed by you |

Cloud plans also differ in event volume, data retention, and the number of websites and team members. See [pricing](https://umami.is/pricing) for current limits. Each feature page in these docs notes the version in which the feature became available.

## Privacy

- **No cookies.** The tracker does not use cookies.
- **No automatic personal data collection.** Umami does not collect personally identifiable information by default. Data you choose to send, such as a distinct ID or event properties, is your responsibility.
- **Your data stays yours.** When you self-host, your analytics data lives on your own infrastructure.

See the [FAQ](/docs/faq) for common privacy questions.
