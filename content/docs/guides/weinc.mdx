[WeInc](https://we.inc) is an AI website builder with a built-in Umami Cloud add-on, so you don't need to paste the tracking code yourself. You only need your website ID.

## Setup

1. In Umami, click **Edit** on the website you want to track and open the **Tracking code** section (see [Collect data](/docs/collect-data)). Copy the `data-website-id` value, for example `fba6be18-1e62-4f4c-9e6a-c0b75cccb7ce`.
2. In WeInc, open your site in the **Editor** and open the **Add-ons** panel.
3. Find **Umami Cloud** under Analytics and click **Turn on**.
4. Paste your website ID into the **Website ID** field and click **Save**.
5. Publish your site. The Umami script is added to the `<head>` of every page on the published site.

Then visit your published site and data should appear in your Umami dashboard. If it doesn't, see the troubleshooting notes in [Collect data](/docs/collect-data).
