[podway](https://podway.io) is a managed hosting service for open-source apps. It runs Umami in its own pod, 24/7, on EU-based infrastructure (Hetzner/OVH). Umami runs with PostgreSQL in its own pod, so there is no separate database to set up.

You can start free with one app on a Mini pod, no card needed. After that, pricing is from $4 per month per app.

Two things are specific to podway:

- **Safe upgrades.** podway takes a snapshot before every upgrade and rolls back automatically if the new version fails its health check.
- **An AI admin.** Setup, upgrades and investigating problems can be handled by an assistant you steer from Claude.

## Deploy Umami

[![Deploy on podway](https://podway.io/img/deploy-on-podway.svg)](https://podway.io/start?app=umami&ref=docs-umami)

1. Click the button above.
2. Sign in to podway or create an account, then follow the steps to launch Umami.
3. When the pod is up, open its URL from your podway dashboard.

Log in with the [default credentials](/docs/login) and change the password immediately.

## Start collecting data

1. Follow [Add a website](/docs/add-a-website) to register your website in Umami.
2. Follow [Collect data](/docs/collect-data) to add the tracking script to your website.
3. Visit your website and confirm that activity appears in the Umami dashboard.

<Callout title="Notes">

- Data is stored in the pod's PostgreSQL database on a persistent volume.
- Custom domains are available for public pods after domain setup.
- You do not run the [update procedure](/docs/updates) yourself: podway upgrades the pod, taking a snapshot first and rolling back automatically if the new version fails its health check.

</Callout>
