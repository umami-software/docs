---
title: Preferences
description: Set your default date range, timezone, 12-hour or 24-hour time format, language, and theme for the Umami dashboard.
---

Preferences change how the Umami dashboard looks and reads for you. They are saved in your browser, so they apply
to the browser you set them in and do not affect other users.

To open them, click on the side nav profile button, click **Settings**, then navigate to **Preferences**.

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Default date range** | The date range selected when you open a website. |
| **Timezone** | The timezone used to display dates and group data. |
| **Time format** | Whether times are shown with a 12-hour or 24-hour clock. **Language default** uses the convention of your selected language. Available since v3.5.0. |
| **Language** | The language of the dashboard. |
| **Theme** | Light or dark mode. |
| **Version** | The version of Umami you are running. |

The date range, timezone, time format, and language settings each have a **Reset** button that restores the default.
